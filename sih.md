# Why the Internal Round is Harder Than It Appears

Many students underestimate the internal evaluation.

However, it is actually one of the toughest filters because:

- Blind review
- Asynchronous evaluation
- No live presentation
- No Q&A
- No opportunity to clarify misunderstandings

Judges only evaluate:

- PPT
- Documentation
- Architecture
- Problem understanding
- Technical feasibility

Unlike the national round, there is:

- No pitching ability
- No personality advantage
- No mentor intervention

Everything depends solely on the quality of the submitted document.

# What Actually Wins the Blind Round

## 1. Follow the Official PPT Template

Do **not** redesign the format.

Follow the official template exactly.

---

## 2. Deep Problem Understanding

Avoid generic ideas like:

> "AI Chatbot for Everything"

Instead demonstrate:

- Specific problem
- Real stakeholders
- Existing pain points
- Supporting statistics
- Government relevance

---

## 3. Feasible Scope

Judges prefer:

- Smaller
- Realistic
- Buildable

rather than

- Huge
- Impossible
- Overly ambitious

---

## 4. Show Technical Thinking

Include:

- Architecture Diagram
- Workflow
- Technology Stack
- Modules
- Data Flow

This signals implementation readiness.

---

## 5. Quantify Impact

Clearly define:

- Target users
- Beneficiaries
- Expected improvements
- Existing process limitations
- Measurable outcomes
